02Entrepreneurship / Real-world experience

Pinoy Party Rentals

Learning how a business works, one booking at a time.

Since homeschooling, I've been helping out with my family's party rental business in Auckland.

Depending on the day, I might be handling enquiries, tracking finances, making content, organising inventory, working on marketing, helping with deliveries, or experimenting with ways to improve our systems.

It's given me a much better understanding of what actually goes into running a small business.
